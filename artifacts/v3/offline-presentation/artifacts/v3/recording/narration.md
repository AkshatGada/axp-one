Original run: v3-wallet-acceptance; original network: hosted Solana sandbox. Presentation is recorded evidence replay. The MP4 holds actual browser frames for explanation; it does not depict fresh execution. No microphone or generated voice.

## 00:00:00 — foundation

AXP.one is an advertising exchange for the agentic internet. Advertiser agents evaluate conversational opportunities; deterministic code chooses an eligible winner. The publisher delivers a separate Sponsored card, and accepted receipts drive stablecoin accounting. This is a recorded replay of our completed V3 hosted-Solana-sandbox run, not fresh execution, Devnet or mainnet.

## 00:01:45 — decisions
