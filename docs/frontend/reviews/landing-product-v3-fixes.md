# Landing V3: review and fix list (11 October 2026)

Two independent read-only reviews of /v3/: design and motion (craft 7/10, cohesion 5.5/10) and a
cold reader as marketer (6/10) and AI-app builder (7/10), plus a truth and scope check. One bounded
fixes pass follows; result is tagged `axp-landing-product-v3.1`.

## Protect
The hero wall, dive and exploded stack; the moment's held card; the advertiser's Bitcoin beat,
Edit/Approve and Pause; Tidewater and its answer hold; the agreement diagram; the doors panel.

## Integrator (shared), done before the builders start
- Receipt-parting seam removed (it duplicated the receipt, never reached the doors, widened phones).
- Tile seam rebuilt: wide screens only, stays inside its own band, rests visibly with motion off.
- One switch language in v9.css (`.v8-sw`): rules are square ink latches, the judgment is round.
- Less air on short laptop screens (`--v8-pad` under 820px height).
- Copy: wall note names brands as illustrative; the stack's repeated caption removed; the stack's
  money plate is "Charge", not "Payment"; header button says "Advertiser dashboard".
- Footer: tagline beside the links, no empty left half.

## Opening
Bid beams cross the headline and wall text; the wall note sits on a tile. "Passes" misreads
(use "Sits out"); "its ceiling" is jargon. The stack has no illustration label. Plate labels are
small and skewed at reading speed. The gathered deck lingers as a sliver over the next scene.
The dive zigzags. Phones: six full plates are about 1,500px with dead space after.
Fewer "Delivered" marks on background tiles.

## The moment
The specimen is under-scaled beside the headline. Reason tags are the point and too small.
Say whose judgment it is (the advertiser's agent), so the next heading lands. Autoplay must pause
when the scene is mostly out of view and always rest on the first question. Phones: picker and
result must share a view.

## The advertiser's side
Wires need their own lane and a trunk; the blocked wire must end on its tile. Entry is too fast
(about 15 things in 750ms): stage it over about 2.5s. Verdicts are the point and the smallest type.
Cap multi-capability reasons to one short line. Heading at the shared size. No "USDC" and no
figures on the bounds (reads as a crypto price): two labelled bounds. Plainer words than
"May join" and "declared" where possible. Lighter chips on phones. Use the shared rule switch.

## The app's side
Two of four switches do nothing visible. "Network: Qualifying ad" and "You carry" are jargon.
The "nothing is shown" message belongs inside the empty slot, not as a toast on the bar.
Fit question and controls in one view at 1366x768 and 1280x800. Phones: the control and the card
in one view. Opener matches the other scenes. Use the shared rule switch.

## Where they meet, and the doors
Opener matches the other scenes (left, shared grid, 80px margin). Receipt must be in view with the
heading at 1440x900 and 1366x768. Fold the caveats into the receipt object; put the proof label
inside the link. Phones: fix the broken stub connector in the "outside limits" state. Use the
shared switches.

## Not in this pass
The preview server does not serve the product routes (dashboard, chat, guide): unchanged.
The separate /demo/ walkthrough page still says "Live product." and names providers: owner's call.
